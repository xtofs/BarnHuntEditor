Course Layout

Bales and other items can be edited with the keyboard or the mouse.

  • Drag items to move
  • ↓ ↑ ← → to move selected elements
  • Shift for smaller movements
  • r to rotate a bale
  • a to toggle anchor
  • 1 2 3 to change the level of bale
  • Del to remove selected element
